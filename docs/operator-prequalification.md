operators and drilling contractors active in the Orange Basin and offshore SA.

**business-development** workstream (it wins work), with backlink side-benefits —
not primarily SEO. Owner: Afrishore. Verify every URL/email before acting.

Mira/Bollsta/Hercules programmes). Formal prequalification converts project-by-

## Single highest-leverage action: Achilles Global Energy

**Odfjell Drilling** — Afrishore's most frequent client — uses **Achilles Global
Energy** (formerly FPAL) as its screening tool when building Invitation-to-Tender
lists, and it also covers **Northern Ocean** (whose fleet Odfjell manages). One
registration makes Afrishore discoverable to Odfjell, Northern Ocean, and any
other Achilles-subscribing operator.

- Platform: achilles.com → Global Energy community (NOT Magnet JQS, which is Norway/Denmark only)
- Entry: **self-registration** (or buyer invitation). Select marine-logistics / vessel-agency product-service codes at 3rd + 4th level.
- Steps: register → complete questionnaire (HSE, financials, certifications) → subscribe & pay → profile goes visible to buyers. Consider the **Achilles Verify** audit — Odfjell specifically values it for high-risk marine ops.
- Also: accept Odfjell's Supplier Code of Conduct (Doc L1-CORP-015).
- Cost: paid subscription (annual, auto-renew). **Priority: do first.**

## Local-content & registration context

**Namibia** — MME National Upstream Petroleum Local Content Policy (final draft, March 2025): operators must submit Local Content Plans and the regulator will maintain a **local-supplier database**; all contracts advertised locally. No fixed % target yet. *Afrishore is already strong here* — Namibian registration + a permanent Walvis Bay presence inside the port's Oil & Gas Section is exactly the local-content footprint the policy rewards. **Action:** register in the MME supplier database when it goes live (monitor announcements).

**South Africa** — Upstream Petroleum Resources Development Act 23 of 2024: local-content plans, procurement-progression plans for black-owned suppliers, and **B-BBEE** as a procurement gatekeeper (PPPFA 80/20 — up to 20 preference points; Level 1 = max).

> ⚠️ **B-BBEE guardrail.** B-BBEE is a genuine SA procurement lever, but whether Afrishore pursues a level is a **shareholder/commercial decision for Chris** — not something to action off a research report. And per our standing rule, **do not state any B-BBEE level on the website or in any listing until one is actually held.** Flag for discussion; don't claim.
