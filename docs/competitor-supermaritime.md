# Competitor teardown — Supermaritime Group (vessel/ship agency, Cape Town & SA)

**Tier 3 — internal only.** Competitor benchmarking; shapes work, never appears in
public copy. Compiled 2026-08-13 from live research (three parallel passes: SERP,
off-site authority, on-page) + Afrishore GSC. Supermaritime was not previously in
the benchmark (docs tracked GAC / Inchcape / Manica-OLS / Rennies).

## Verdict

It is **not an on-page problem — Afrishore out-builds them.** Where Supermaritime
ranks above Afrishore it is on three off-page forces: **domain age, entity authority,
and the local map-pack.** The premise is also only half-true: on the "vessel" phrasing
Afrishore already ranks ahead; Supermaritime wins the "ship agency" phrasing and the
localised google.co.za result (map-pack + .co.za weighting).

**Afrishore GSC (real SA searchers, 90 days):** "vessel agency" pos ~10 / 1 impression;

town" pos 39. Brand "afrishore cape town" pos 1.1. **25% of impressions are brand** —
Google knows Afrishore as a name, not yet the category authority.

## Why Supermaritime wins where it wins

1. **Domain age.** supermaritime.com registered **2000** (26 yrs); afrishore.co
   **Jun 2024** (~2 yrs). The .co.za→.co migration reset the domain-age signal.
   Only time fixes this — **never migrate the domain again.**
2. **Entity authority / citation density.** 1989 multinational; Crunchbase, ZoomInfo,
   Craft.co, per-country MarineTraffic profiles, LinkedIn 3,100+ followers, 30+
   interlinked office pages under one domain. Afrishore reads as a small, thin entity.
3. **Local map-pack.** Physical in-harbour Cape Town office (FPT Building, D-Berth,
   South Arm Road, Cape Town Harbour) → strong GBP signal. On localised "…cape town"
   searches the map-pack sits above organic.

## Their glass jaw (Afrishore's opening — their on-page is objectively worse)

- Title tags keyword-free ("Cape Town - Supermaritime Group"); H1s brand-led
  ("Supermaritime Cape Town"). No commercial keyword in any title.
- **Zero JSON-LD schema. No meta descriptions. No canonicals. No Open Graph.** Dated
  Joomla, ~300-word duplicated city pages (same paragraph verbatim on SA + Cape Town),
  sitemap 403s, pinch-zoom disabled.
- **Only two SA city pages** (Cape Town, Durban) — none for Saldanha, Gqeberha,
  Richards Bay, Mossel Bay.

Afrishore's Cape Town page: 1,079 words, keyword-led title/H1, full Organization/
LocalBusiness/Service schema, canonical, in sitemap. Afrishore is the modern build —
the "out-modernise the incumbents" play. As the domain ages and citations build, the
quality tells.

## Fix list (leverage × effort)

1. **GBP per office, Cape Town first** — highest-leverage; directly attacks their
   map-pack win. Category "Ship agent" + "Logistics service", NAP identical to site.
   (Already flagged "untouched, highest local lever" in geo-offsite-checklist.)
2. **Capture the "ship agency / ship agent" phrasing** — Afrishore ranks for "vessel
   agent/agency" but is absent for "ship agency". Weave the synonym naturally into the
   Cape Town page + /services/ship-rig-agency/ titles and body. Cheap; ranks adjacent.
3. **Entity/citation density** — deliberately replicate their passive footprint via
   FREE listings only (**Crunchbase is paid now — do NOT recommend it**, Chris
   declined 2026-08-13): Kompass (free basic B2B, already on the directory list),
   Craft.co / ZoomInfo (free claim of the auto-generated profile), complete LinkedIn
   page, enrich MarineTraffic to all ports, + TNPA vessel-agent registration and the
   SA directories in legitimacy-registrations.md. Identical NAP everywhere.
4. **Own the ports they've ceded** — Supermaritime has only Cape Town + Durban;
   Afrishore already has Saldanha, Mossel Bay, Lüderitz. Win the long tail.
5. **Domain age** — un-rushable: keep afrishore.co stable forever.

## Directory / citation targets ranking on page 1 (from SERP pass)

- **shipchandlers.co.za/south-african-shipping-agents/** — ranks on all 5 queries.
  Afrishore IS listed (contact Charles Taute); Supermaritime is NOT. Enrich the entry.
- marinevesseltraffic.com, shippingexplorer.net — carry Rennies listings (incumbents
  rank *through* directories, not their own pages).
- magicport.ai/ship-agents/south-africa — national; Afrishore on it (enrich).
- theafricalogistics.com "6 best shipping agents in South Africa" — page 1 listicle;
  Afrishore not in it — pitch for inclusion.
- goodfirms.co, swiftwl.co.za — page 1.

Directory nuance: on the two SA/NA agent directories readable (Namport, shipchandlers),
**Afrishore appears and Supermaritime does not** — so directory presence is NOT why
Supermaritime outranks. The edge is domain age + entity authority + local office.
